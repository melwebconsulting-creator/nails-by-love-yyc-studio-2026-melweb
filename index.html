BIAB (Builder in a Bottle)

3 – 4+ Weeks Retention

Structured builder gel overlay applied directly onto natural nails. Adds strength, balances natural nail curves, and creates a reinforced apex so your natural nails can grow long and healthy without bending or snapping.

  • Ideal for nail biters or weak, flexible nails
  • Can be infilled every 3–4 weeks for continuous growth
  • Stunning milky nude, blush pink, and sheer neutral shades
  • Includes Russian/dry cuticle care and reinforcement

What to Include in Your DM:

  • ✓ Inspiration Photos: Clear photos showing the design and shape you want
  • ✓ Service Type: GelX Extensions, BIAB Natural Overlay, or Shellac
  • ✓ Desired Length: Short, Medium, Long, or Extra Long
  • ✓ Current Nails: Let us know if you currently have existing gel, acrylic, or foreign work to remove
  • ✓ Availability: Your preferred days of the week and times of day

Studio Location

Located in NE Calgary, Alberta in a quiet, hygienic private studio atmosphere. Full address and parking instructions are provided upon confirmed booking.

Arrival & Punctuality

Please arrive on time. If you anticipate being more than 10 minutes late, please send a message as early as possible so we can adjust or reschedule.

Clean & Bare Nails

For new sets, please arrive with bare nails unless you specifically booked a soak-off removal in advance. We do not work over damaged, infected, or severely lifted foreign acrylic without safe removal.

GelX uses full-cover soft gel tips applied over your nail with gel adhesive to add instant length and shape. BIAB (Builder in a Bottle) is a thick structured gel applied directly to your natural nail to reinforce it and allow it to grow long on its own without tip extensions.
Because nail art complexity, crystal embellishments, and length vary, sending a screenshot of your inspiration photo to @nailsbylove_yyc on Instagram allows us to give you an exact, transparent quote before your visit.
A standard Shellac manicure takes approximately 60 minutes. BIAB overlays take roughly 75–90 minutes. GelX extensions with intricate custom nail art typically range from 90 to 120 minutes for immaculate precision.
We accept Interac e-Transfer and cash. Payment is due upon completion of your appointment.
